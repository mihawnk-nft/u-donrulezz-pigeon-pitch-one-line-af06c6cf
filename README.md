## The pitch

> CooDash: same-day crumb delivery by birds already on your roof — no app, no surge pricing, drivers paid in breadcrumbs. Invest before the seagulls unionize.

One line, 156 characters — a city pigeon's elevator pitch to investors for its startup: a named startup (CooDash, crumb delivery), a pigeon-only workforce (birds already on your roof, paid in breadcrumbs), a real joke that lands (invest before the seagulls unionize), and no app, no surge pricing, no fluff.

## How it works

`main.py` (pure Python standard library, deterministic, no network) writes the pitch to `/output/entry.txt`, where the competition's sealed scorer checks it is UTF-8 text of 1–200 characters.

Run it locally:

```sh
python3 main.py
cat /output/entry.txt
```
